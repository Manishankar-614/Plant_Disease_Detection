Model & System Architecture

Multi-Task Deep Learning Engine

FloraScan AI deploys a unified convolutional neural network that simultaneously decodes plant anatomy and identifies pathological conditions from raw imagery.

MobileNetV2 Backbone

Utilizes inverted residual structures and linear bottlenecks pre-trained on ImageNet to extract rich botanical features with ultra-low compute latency.

Dual-Output Heads

A single forward pass concurrently evaluates two specialized Softmax heads: Part Classification (Leaves, Stems, Fruits) and Pathology Detection.

Real-Time Inference

Optimized for sub-50ms execution on standard CPUs, making edge deployment, camera feeds, and mobile field analysis responsive and seamless.
